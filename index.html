About Me

Who I Am

I'm Aleeza Ibad, a Flutter & WordPress developer focused on shipping clean, production-ready mobile apps and websites that solve real business problems.

What draws me to mobile development is the craft of turning complex workflows into interfaces people can use without friction — and watching an idea become something that lives on a client's phone every day.

I build with Flutter & Dart, Firebase, and AI integrations — delivering 6+ apps from AI chat to fitness planning through the Bano Qabil program. As a certified WordPress developer, I deployed multiple client sites during my Frontend Intern role at NEXCRAFTSTUDIO in 2025.

RateBridge is a production Flutter app I built for Usman Associates, a construction business in Rawalpindi — with multi-role access, Firebase, Google Gemini AI, and MVVM/Provider architecture. I hold a BS in Information Technology from the University of Punjab with a CGPA of 3.64/4.0.
